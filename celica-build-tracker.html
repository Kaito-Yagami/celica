Using the schematic prompts

Each stage carries a prompt written for an image model. Three things make the difference between a usable schematic and a poster:

  • Ask for numbered callouts, not words. Image models garble long labels. The prompts request circled numerals; you add the legend yourself.
  • Generate two or three and keep the clearest. Geometry varies a lot between seeds on technical subjects.
  • Treat the result as a sketch, not a drawing. It's for thinking and for showing someone what you mean — dimensions still come from your own measurements.
